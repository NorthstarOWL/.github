<h1 align="center">Northstar Owl</h1>

<p align="center">Software for building a life in the UK. One account across every product.</p>

| Product | For |
|---------|-----|
| **WorthyRole** | Finding roles worth applying for in the UK: each posting read against your CV, visa sponsorship checked, a tailored CV for the ones that fit |
| **Settle** | Moving to the UK with your family: visa dates, GP registration, qualification recognition, school places. Information, not immigration advice |
| **Money** | Households keeping bills in check: new deals compared with what you already hold, renewals tracked. Information, not financial advice |
| **Accounts** | Sole traders and small companies: bookkeeping and tax deadlines kept in order through the year |
| **Home** | Renting or buying in the UK: listings checked against budget, lease length, flood zone, EPC rating and commute |

Your details, documents and deadlines carry over between products, and nothing moves from one product to another until you say so.
